A small onepage website about Tihar, the festival of lights celebrated in Nepal. It walks through the five days of the festival and a few things worth knowing about each one, like why crows are honored on the first day and what the colors in a Bhai Tika really mean . I initially thought about making a cofee landing page, but thought why not make it about one of the most important festivals in my country.

- What's on the page
A short count of Tihar facts
Highlights: Kukur Tihar, Laxmi Puja and Bhai Tika
An about section on Tihar, also called Deepawali or Yamapanchak

Files
index.html - the page structure
style.css - all the styling
script.js - for hamburger menu to open

No build step and nothing to install to use it. Its a simple webpage. Head on over to https://tihar-tau.vercel.app.
