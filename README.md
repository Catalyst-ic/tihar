A small one-page website about Tihar, the festival of lights celebrated in Nepal. It walks through the five days of the festival and a few things worth knowing about each one, like why crows are honored on the first day and what the colors in a Bhai Tika mean.
It started as a coffee shop landing page. We kept the layout and styling and rewrote everything else to be about Tihar.

- What's on the page
A short count of Tihar facts
Highlights: Kukur Tihar, Laxmi Puja and Bhai Tika
An about section on Tihar, also called Deepawali or Yamapanchak

Files
index.html - the page structure
style.css - all the styling
script.js - the typing effect on the headline

No build step and nothing to install to use it. Its a simple webpage. Head on over to https://tihar-tau.vercel.app.
